### Selected work

- **Cloud-native SfM for IARPA WRIVA** — wide-baseline, mixed-altitude imagery fused with GPS, OpenStreetMap and satellite priors. #1 on the WRIVA leaderboard. [demo](https://cpeng93.github.io/JHU-in-3D/)
- **Ground → satellite geolocalization via 3DGS** — ground images rendered to a zenith view with Gaussian Splatting and Difix3D, then registered to WorldView-3 imagery. Sub-10 m camera RMSE; #1 in the IARPA WRIVA Cross-View Geo-Localization Challenge 2026. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14929)
- **Campus-scale reconstruction from drone footage** — UCSB campus reconstructed end-to-end from thousands of heterogeneous drone images. [demo 1](https://youtu.be/2GJS7BRKg1s) · [demo 2](https://youtu.be/LbreY2Czc7E)
- **MetaReg: fast incremental registration** — custom PnP using noisy GPS and spatial neighbours to add a camera to an existing reconstruction in under 1.34 s. [ICIP 2025](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pQgsJrdcIrL6Ntq7tu0qqvlFOJ5Z3w9F/view)
